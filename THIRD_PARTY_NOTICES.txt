Converted to 22050 Hz mono PCM in assets/audio/zombie-moan-{1,2,3}.wav,
and embedded in zombie-voices.js for offline/file URL compatibility.
These are independent recordings, not audio extracted from Minecraft.

Animal and material recordings (2026-11-24)
------------------------------------------
Distributed as unchanged OGG recordings in assets/audio/world, and as lossless
base64 embeddings in world-audio-data.js. Playback applies gain, pitch and
filtering in memory. Original files and their licenses remain unchanged.
Source repository: https://github.com/VoxeLibre/VoxeLibre
Exact source revision and filenames: assets/audio/world/source.json
Upstream credits: assets/audio/world/mobs-license.md and blocks-license.txt
These recordings and our original procedural textures are not Mojang assets.
