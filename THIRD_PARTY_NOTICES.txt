Zombie voice recordings: "Zombie Moans 01 - By EmoPreben" by EmoPreben.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-moans-01-by-emopreben
License: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Converted to 22050 Hz mono PCM in assets/audio/zombie-moan-{1,2,3}.wav,
and embedded in zombie-voices.js for offline/file URL compatibility.
These are independent recordings, not audio extracted from Minecraft.
